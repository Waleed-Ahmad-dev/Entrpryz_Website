COMMON QUESTIONS
[Heading3] About our services
Q: How long does an ERP implementation take?
It depends on scope, modules and how ready your data is. We give a realistic timeline after the Understand and Analyze steps, not before.
Q: Can you improve an ERP we already own?
Yes. Many of our engagements start with an existing system that is under-used, over-customized or poorly integrated.
Q: Do you build custom software as well as implement ERP?
Yes. Our team covers both, so integrations and custom modules are planned together with the ERP, not bolted on later.
Q: Is Odoo a good fit for a small or mid-size business?
Often, yes. Odoo covers accounting, sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing and CRM in one system, and it can be introduced one area at a time. We will tell you honestly if your operation is better served by another platform.
Q: What is the difference between Odoo Community and Odoo Enterprise?
Community is the open-source edition with no license fee and covers the core business apps. Enterprise is the licensed edition with additional apps, vendor-hosted options and vendor support. We help you choose based on the features you actually need.
Q: What does an Odoo project cost?
Odoo Community has no license fee, so the cost sits in implementation, customization, hosting and support. We scope these after a short call so you see the full picture before you commit.
Q: Can Odoo be customized and connected to our other systems?
Yes. Odoo can be extended with custom modules and connected to your website, banks, courier services and other software. We keep customizations clean so future upgrades stay manageable.

WHAT AN AI AGENT DOES
[Heading3] Practical help for tasks that eat your team's day
An AI Agent is software that can take a task, work through the steps and hand back a result. It reads the incoming document or message, checks it against your data, and prepares the next action for a person to approve.
[Heading4] Handle incoming enquiries
Answer common questions, collect the missing details and pass qualified requests to the right person.
[Heading4] Read and check documents
Pull the key fields from invoices, purchase orders and delivery notes, and flag anything that does not match your records.
[Heading4] Answer questions about your data
Ask a plain-language question about stock, sales or receivables and get an answer from your system, with the source shown.
[Heading4] Prepare reports and summaries
Draft the weekly numbers, exception lists and management summaries your team now assembles by hand.
[Heading4] Follow up and remind
Chase overdue approvals, payments and replies so nothing waits for someone to remember.
[Heading4] Triage and route work
Sort requests by type and urgency and send them to the person who should deal with them.

COMMON QUESTIONS
[Heading3] About AI Agents
Q: What is an AI Agent?
An AI Agent is software that takes a task, works through the steps using your data and tools, and returns a result for a person to review or approve. It is different from a simple chatbot because it can act on information in your systems.
Q: Will an AI Agent replace my staff?
That is not our goal. Agents take over repetitive tasks such as checking, copying and chasing, so your team has time for work that needs judgement. People review the results and make the decisions.
Q: Is my business data safe?
We agree up front which data the agent can see, where it is processed and who can access the results. We use only the data the task needs, and we build access controls and logs into every agent.
Q: Can an agent work with our ERP?
Yes. We connect agents to platforms such as NetSuite, SAP Business One, Odoo and Dynamics 365, as well as email, documents and WhatsApp, depending on what your process uses.
Q: How do we start?
Send a short note about the task that costs you the most time. We will talk it through, tell you honestly whether an agent is the right answer, and propose a small pilot if it is.
